✦ Our Story ✦

Two shops. One thread.

The original O'Malley Fabrics still trades in Ballina, County Mayo, where my mother Anne has kept the door open — and the kettle on — since 1978.

The Original — Ireland

O'Malley Fabrics

Ballina, Co. Mayo

A fabric shop and sewing room on the banks of the River Moy. Bolts of Irish linen, Donegal tweed, and lace stacked floor to ceiling — and a counter where half the town's wedding dresses, communion gowns, and confirmation suits have been pinned, taken in, and let out for nearly five decades.

Est. 1978 · Anne O'Malley
The Studio — USA

O'Malley Fabrics

Bethesda, Maryland

The American chapter of the same story. I grew up threading needles under my mother's worktable; today I run a by-appointment studio serving the DC, Maryland, and Virginia area — with linen and tweed shipped across the Atlantic from the original shop's shelves.

Est. 2019 · Grainne O'Malley

✦ How It Works ✦

From first pin to final press.

i

Consult

Bring the garment (and the shoes you'll wear with it). We talk fit, fabric, and timeline.

ii

Pin & Measure

Everything is pinned on your body, never guessed from a chart. You approve before I cut.

iii

Stitch

Hand-finishing where it matters, machine precision where it counts. Updates by text.

iv

Press & Fit

A final fitting and professional press. It doesn't leave the studio until it's right.
