    • Grainne O'Malley

    Grainne grew up in the west of Ireland surrounded by beautiful fabrics, buttons, and all kinds of haberdashery in her parents fabric store, O'Malley Fabrics. Grainne's mother Anne is a highly skilled seamstress making everything from wedding dresses to bringing the local fashion designers sketches to life. Grainne inherited her mother's love for fabric and altering garments to custom fit the wearer.

    From her current Bethesda home Grainne continues the skills she inherited to alter customers clothes to custom fit them and brighten their home decor with customized pillows and curtains.

    With O'Malley Fabrics, my aim is to carry on that beautiful legacy. Whether you need a simple alteration to make an outfit feel just right, custom curtains to brighten your home, or pillows to add that perfect finishing touch, I'm here to help.

    It would be my pleasure to assist you with your sewing needs, big or small, and share a piece of my heritage with you.
