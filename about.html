Grainne O'Malley — Founder & Master Seamstress

Craftsmanship & Heritage

Craftsmanship Rooted in Heritage

Grainne O'Malley grew up in County Mayo, Ireland, surrounded by fine fabrics and the craft of tailoring in her family's beloved fabric store. Inspired by her mother Anne — an accomplished seamstress known for exquisite wedding dresses and bespoke designs — Grainne developed a deep passion for quality textiles and precision garment alteration.

Now based in Bethesda, Maryland, Grainne brings that same dedication to detail and craftsmanship to every project. She specialises in custom garment alterations that ensure a perfect fit, as well as bespoke home décor solutions including custom curtains and tailored pillows.

O'Malley Fabrics honours this family legacy of excellence. Whether you need a professional alteration to perfect your wardrobe, custom window treatments to enhance your home, or decorative pillows to add a refined finishing touch, Grainne works with you to bring your vision to life.

With meticulous attention to detail and a genuine passion for quality, she is committed to delivering exceptional results on every project, no matter the scale.
